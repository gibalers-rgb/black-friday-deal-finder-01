How to read the verdict

  • ★ NEW LOW — the lowest price the cited source has ever tracked for this product.
  • ● STRONG DEAL — a deep cut: 25% or more off, or a large dollar saving against the usual price.
  • ○ ROUTINE SALE — a real discount, but a small one. Worth knowing before you buy, not a reason to rush.
